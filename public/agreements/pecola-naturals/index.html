01. Project Overview

The project, titled "Pecola Naturals Online Store", involves creating a fully custom Shopify storefront for Pecola Naturals, a black-and-white branded skincare business specialising in natural, non-toxic, vegan body butters. The primary goal is to deliver a premium, minimal online shopping experience that reflects the Pecola Naturals brand identity and gives the Client full control over orders, customers and product management from day one.

02. Scope of Work

  • Full website design and front-end build, including complete Shopify store setup
  • Custom branding, black-and-white identity, refined typography, considered product photography placement
  • Product setup, initial catalogue of up to 7 products uploaded with content supplied by the Client
  • Payment integration, Shopify payment gateway and shipping configuration
  • Shopify admin configuration so the Client can seamlessly track orders, customer data and sales in one place
  • Mobile optimisation, fully responsive across mobile, tablet and desktop
  • Guidance, on managing products, orders, customers and the Shopify admin after handover

Anything outside this scope requires written agreement.

03. Deliverables

Upon completion, the Client will receive:

  • A fully functional custom-built Shopify storefront with integrated online shop
  • Custom branded design across home, shop, product, about, contact, and order tracking pages
  • Initial product catalogue setup (up to 7 products from supplied content)
  • Payment and shipping systems configured for the Shopify store
  • Shopify admin configured for clear order tracking, customer data visibility and product management
  • Guidance and ongoing help on how to manage orders and product information

Website Functionality & Custom Features

The Developer will implement features and functionality designed to meet the needs outlined in the Scope of Work. The exact structure and capabilities of any custom feature will be based on the data and requirements provided by the Client and the technical capabilities of the chosen platform. The Client acknowledges that:

  • Accurate output depends on correct data, tagging, and organisation provided by the Client
  • Ongoing management of content, data, and configuration is the Client's responsibility after handover
  • Advanced or custom features beyond the agreed scope may require additional development and cost

04. Hosting, Ownership & Website Transfer

Development & Hosting Environment

All work produced by the Developer, including but not limited to website design, code, configurations, and content, remains the sole property of the Developer until full and final payment has been received. Until such payment is made, the Client is granted a limited, non-transferable, and revocable licence to access the website for review purposes only. The Client shall have no ownership rights prior to full payment.

Transfer of Ownership

Upon receipt of full and final payment, the Developer will transfer the website or application to an account or environment owned and controlled by the Client. Ownership of the website and its associated assets will pass to the Client only upon successful completion of this transfer.

Client Account & Ongoing Costs

The Client is responsible for:

  • Creating or providing the appropriate hosting or platform account in their own name for transfer
  • Selecting and paying for any required platform subscription, hosting plan, or service fees
  • Paying for any third-party apps, themes, plugins, services, or APIs required for the live website
  • Managing domain registration and associated costs unless otherwise agreed in writing

The Client acknowledges that certain features or applications used during development may require separate purchase or subscription under their own account after transfer.

Handover Process

The Developer will initiate the transfer of the website within a reasonable timeframe after final payment is received. The Client agrees to accept the transfer promptly and within fourteen (14) days of notification. Failure to do so does not delay the transfer of responsibility.

Post-Transfer Responsibility

Once the website has been transferred, the Client assumes full responsibility for:

  • Hosting, platform subscription, and billing
  • Website management, updates, and maintenance
  • Security, backups, and compliance

The Developer shall have no ongoing responsibility or liability for the website after transfer, unless a separate maintenance or support agreement has been agreed in writing.

Access & Permissions

During development, the Developer may provide the Client with preview or limited access for feedback purposes. Administrative or ownership-level access will not be granted until full payment has been received and the transfer process is complete.

Backup & Portfolio Rights

The Developer reserves the right to retain a copy of the website for backup, record-keeping, and portfolio purposes, unless otherwise agreed in writing.
