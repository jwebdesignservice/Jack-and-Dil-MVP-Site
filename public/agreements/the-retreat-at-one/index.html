01. Project Overview

The project, titled "Social Media Post Designs for The Retreat @one", involves the creation of custom, on-brand social media post designs for The Retreat @one, a beauty salon, built in Canva as fully editable templates that the salon can manage and publish herself. The primary goal is to give the salon a consistent, professional presence across Instagram and Facebook, with designs ordered flexibly on a pay-per-design basis and no ongoing commitment.

02. Scope of Work

  • Custom, on-brand social media post designs created in Canva
  • A consistent visual style applied across all designs
  • Individual post designs covering the salon's treatments and promotions, including facials (CACI and Eve Taylor), HD brows, lash lifts, brow lamination, and a "coming soon" teaser for the new Japanese head spa
  • An optional editable offers/notes header for promoting current offers and announcements
  • Placeholder image and video frames built into each design for the Client's own photos and clips
  • Delivery of all designs in a shared Canva folder, ready to edit and schedule
  • Designs ordered individually on a pay-per-design basis, with no minimum order, retainer, or ongoing commitment

Anything outside this scope requires written agreement.

03. Deliverables

For each design ordered, the Client will receive:

  • A branded, fully editable Canva post design in a consistent, on-brand style
  • Built-in placeholder image and video frames ready for the Client's own content
  • Editable text and, where applicable, an editable offers/notes header
  • The finished design delivered to a shared Canva folder, ready to edit and schedule

Across the engagement, a consistent visual style is maintained across all designs, so the salon's social media stays cohesive as new posts are added over time.

Content & Client Responsibilities

The designs are delivered as branded, fully editable Canva templates. The Client is responsible for populating and publishing them. The Client acknowledges that:

  • The Client supplies all images and videos used in the designs, including her own treatment photos and clips and any supplier product shots or videos obtained from trade portals, and confirms she has the right to use them
  • The Client drops her own content into the placeholder frames and edits any text or offer details before publishing
  • The Client schedules and publishes all posts to Instagram and Facebook from her own accounts
  • Editing and scheduling the designs requires the Client's own Canva account; any paid Canva features the Client chooses to use are her responsibility
  • Work beyond the agreed per-design scope, such as entirely new design styles or additional formats, may require additional design time and cost

04. Ownership, Delivery & Usage

Ownership & Usage Rights

Each design produced by the Developer remains the sole property of the Developer until that design has been paid for in full. Upon payment for a given design, the Client is granted the right to use, edit, publish, and reproduce that design across her own social media channels and marketing. Until payment is received for a design, the Client is granted a limited, non-transferable, and revocable licence to preview it for review purposes only.

Delivery

Designs are delivered as editable Canva templates within a shared Canva folder that the Client can access, edit, and schedule from. The Client is responsible for maintaining her own Canva account in order to access, edit, and publish the designs.

Client-Supplied & Third-Party Content

The Client supplies all photos and videos used within the designs. Any supplier product shots, videos, logos, or other third-party material provided by the Client remains the property of its respective owners and is used under the Client's own licences or permissions. The Client confirms she has the right to use any content she provides, and the Developer accepts no liability for content supplied by the Client.

Platform & Publishing

Editing, scheduling, and publishing of all posts is carried out by the Client through her own Canva and social media accounts. The Developer is not responsible for the performance, reach, or results of any published post, nor for any change to the Canva platform, its features, or its pricing.

Portfolio Rights

The Developer reserves the right to retain copies of the designs and to display them for record-keeping and portfolio purposes, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

05. Timelines

Designs are produced on a per-order basis rather than to a single project deadline. When a design is requested, the Developer will confirm an expected turnaround for that design based on current workload and the content or brief provided by the Client.

  • Each design is started once the Client has confirmed the request and supplied any required brief or content
  • Turnaround is confirmed per design at the time of ordering
  • Delays in the Client providing content, feedback, or approvals may affect delivery times
